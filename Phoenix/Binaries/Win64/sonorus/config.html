Pronunciation
Pronunciation Corrections

Fix TTS mispronunciations by replacing words before they reach the voice engine. One replacement per line: word:replacement (case-insensitive, whole words only). Use | to add an IPA pronunciation: word:replacement|/IPA/. IPA-only entries (word:/IPA/) are stored for future provider support. Test TTS to hear your corrections.
