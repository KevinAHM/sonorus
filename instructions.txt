
  SONORUS - Installation Instructions
  ====================================

  1. Extract the zip file to any folder on your computer, but not your game's folder.
     (Right-click the zip > "Extract All" or use 7-Zip/WinRAR)

  2. Open the extracted folder. You should see:
       install_sonorus.bat
       Phoenix/

  3. Make sure Hogwarts Legacy is closed.

  4. Double-click install_sonorus.bat and follow the prompts.

  That's it! This also works for updating Sonorus to a newer version.

  5. Once installed, you can safely delete the extracted files
     (this folder). Sonorus now lives inside your game directory.

  -----------------------------------------------
  Uninstalling Sonorus
  -----------------------------------------------

  To remove Sonorus, run uninstall_sonorus.bat.
  You can find it in the same zip you used to install.

  The uninstaller will offer to back up your data
  (memories, dialog, settings) before removing anything.
